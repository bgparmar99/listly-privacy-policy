Delete Your Listly Account & Data

Last updated: August 9, 2026

You can delete your Listly account and all associated data at any time, directly in the App. This page explains how, what is removed, and what may be retained.

Delete Your Account in the App

This is the quickest way and needs nothing from us:

In the Listly app

Go to: Settings → Delete account

Then: Review what will happen, type DELETE to confirm

Result: Your account is removed immediately

Before you confirm, the App tells you exactly what will happen to your data — which of your lists will be transferred and to whom, which will be deleted and how many items they contain, and how much money owed to you will be written off. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If You Owe Money in a Shared List

If you currently owe money to another member of a shared list, the App asks you to settle up before deleting. This protects the other members: deleting your account removes those expense records, and doing it while you still owe someone would quietly erase a debt they were relying on.

You can settle up inside the App — including simply recording a payment you have already made outside it — and then delete. Money owed to you never blocks deletion; you are free to write it off, and the App shows you the amount first.

Prefer to Ask Us Instead?

If you cannot access the App, or would rather we did it, email us from the address associated with your Listly account:

Deletion request by email

Email: Orbitralabs@gmail.com

Subject line: Delete My Account

Send from: The email address you used to sign up for Listly

We will verify your identity and process the request. You do not need to provide any additional documentation.

What Gets Deleted

Upon verification, we permanently delete:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile (display name and email address)
  • Lists you created and all their contents
  • Your memberships in shared lists
  • Items, prices, and notes you added
  • Expense splits and settlements you were part of
  • Analytics identifiers associated with your account

What May Be Retained

Lists you created that still have other members are transferred to the longest-standing remaining member, so a household does not lose a list it depends on. Items you added to those lists remain, but are no longer attributed to you. Lists where you were the only member are deleted along with everything in them. Beyond that, information may be retained where required for legal or security purposes, or in anonymised form that can no longer identify you.

How Long It Takes

⚡ In the App: immediate. By email: within 30 days.

Deleting in the App takes effect the moment you confirm — there is no waiting period and no confirmation step from us. If you ask us by email instead, we will confirm by reply once it is done.
