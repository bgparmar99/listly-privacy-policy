1. Who We Are

Listly is operated by Parmar Bharatkumar Govindbhai, based in India. We are the data controller for the personal information we collect through the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information. You can create an account with an email address and password, or by signing in with Google or with Apple. Either way we store your email address and the display name shown to other members. If you use an email and password, the password is stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text; if you sign in with Google or Apple, we never receive a password at all.
  • Sign in with Apple and “Hide My Email”. Apple lets you hide your real email address when you sign in. If you choose that, the address we store is an Apple private relay address ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com, and we never see your real one. Listly works exactly the same either way. Because a relay address is different from your real address, be aware that signing in with Apple using “Hide My Email” creates a separate account from any you may already have under your real address.
  • Lists and items. When you create or join a list, we store the list name, items you add (name, quantity, unit, category, notes, urgency flag), and purchase history (prices, dates, who purchased).
  • Expenses and splits. If you use the splitting feature, we store expense amounts, descriptions, dates, who paid, and how the amount is split between members.
  • Settlements. Money movements you record between members.
  • List memberships. Which lists you belong to, your role (admin or member), and when you joined.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Anonymous usage analytics. We use PostHog to understand how the App is used in aggregate — which screens are visited, which features are used, how often the App is opened. Sensitive text inputs (item names, expense descriptions, notes) are NOT captured. PostHog also derives your approximate location (country and region) from your IP address; your precise location is never collected.
  • Device and session info. Operating system, App version, language, time zone, and an anonymous session identifier.
  • Push notification token. If you turn on notifications, we store a token identifying each device you use, so notifications can be delivered to it. See Push notifications below.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect: your precise location (GPS), contacts, photos, camera or microphone access, calendar, SMS messages, call logs, or advertising identifiers. The App does not show ads.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the core App functionality (create lists, calculate splits and balances, and keep shared lists in sync)
  • Authenticate you when you sign in
  • Show you and other list members your activity within shared lists, and send those updates to their devices as they happen
  • Understand which features are useful and improve the App
  • Communicate with you about important account or service changes (we do not send marketing emails)

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Who We Share Your Information With

4.1 Other List Members — Including Your Spending and Balances

Please read this before joining a shared list. A shared list is not private between you and the App. Every other member of that list can see what you paid for, what you owe, what you are owed, and the amounts — not just totals, but the individual expenses and settlements that make them up.

This is the point of the splitting feature, and it is how the App is useful. It does mean that joining a shared list makes your spending within that list visible to the other people in it.

Specifically, other members of a list you belong to can see:

  • Your display name
  • Items you add or mark as purchased
  • Prices you record
  • Expenses and splits involving you — including the amount, who paid, and how it was divided
  • Settlements between you and other members
  • Your running balance in that list — whether you owe money or are owed money, and how much
  • Activity history within that list

Members of a list do not see your email address, your activity in other lists, or anything outside the list you share with them.

You can leave a list at any time to stop sharing further activity. Data you have already contributed to that list stays with it for the remaining members.

4.2 Live Updates to Other Members' Devices

Listly keeps shared lists in sync in real time. When you add an item, record a price, or log an expense, that change is transmitted immediately to the devices of the other members of that list while they have the App open — it is not merely stored for them to fetch later. The same happens in reverse: their changes appear on your device as they make them.

4.3 Service Providers

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. Each acts as a data processor on our behalf:

  • Supabase (data hosting & database). Stores your account, lists, items, expenses, and related data. Servers are located in Singapore. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • PostHog (product analytics). Receives anonymous usage events and derives approximate location from IP address. PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Google (sign-in, only if you choose it). Google confirms your identity to us; we receive your email address and name. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple (sign-in, only if you choose it). Apple confirms your identity to us; we receive an email address — your real one, or a private relay address if you chose to hide it. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Expo Application Services (app updates and notification delivery). The App checks Expo's servers for over-the-air updates to its own code; Expo receives your device's IP address and the App version as part of that check. If you turn on notifications, Expo also receives your device's push token, the list name, and a short description of what changed, in order to deliver the notification. It does not receive your account credentials, your expense amounts, or your data from lists it is not notifying you about. Expo Privacy Policy
  • Apple Push Notification service (notification delivery on iOS, only if you turn on notifications). Receives the notification and your device token from Expo in order to deliver it to your device. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (notification delivery on Android, only if you turn on notifications). Receives the notification and your device token from Expo in order to deliver it to your device. Google Privacy Policy

5. Push notifications

If you turn on notifications, Listly stores a push notification token for each device you use. This token identifies the device to Apple's and Google's notification services; it does not identify you personally and is not used for advertising or analytics.

We store the token against your account so we can notify you when another member of a shared list adds, buys, or removes an item, joins the list, or records a split or settlement. We never send you notifications about your own actions.

To deliver them we pass the token, the list name, and a short description of what changed to Expo Application Services, which forwards the message to Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers process this information solely to deliver notifications on our behalf.

If you turn notifications off in Listly's Settings, we mark your device's token inactive and stop sending to it; the token is retained so that turning notifications back on does not require setting them up again. Your tokens are deleted when you sign out and when you delete your account. We also remove any token that the notification service reports as no longer registered, which happens if you uninstall the app.

You can turn notifications off at any time in Listly's Settings, or in your device's system settings.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data (email, display name) is retained while your account is active.
  • List and item data is retained while the list exists and you are a member.
  • Analytics events are retained by PostHog for up to 12 months.

When you delete your account from inside the App, the removal happens immediately as part of that action rather than on a delay. Your profile, your list memberships, your expense records and your analytics identifier are deleted at that point.

Lists you created that still have other members are transferred to the longest-standing remaining member, so a household does not lose a list it depends on. Lists where you were the only member are deleted along with everything in them. Items you added to lists that survive remain in those lists, but are no longer attributed to you.

If you ask us to delete your account by email instead, we complete it within 30 days. We may retain limited information where the law requires it.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from inside the App: Settings → Delete account. Before you confirm, the App tells you exactly what will happen to your data — which of your lists will be transferred and to whom, which will be deleted and how many items they hold, and how much money owed to you will be written off. If you would rather not use the App, email us at Orbitralabs@gmail.com and we will do it for you. Full details: deleting your account.

One case where deletion is held up. If you currently owe money to another member of a shared list, the App asks you to settle that before deleting. This exists to protect the other members: deleting your account removes those expense records, and doing it while you still owe someone would quietly erase a debt they were relying on. You can settle up inside the App — including simply recording a payment you already made outside it — and then delete. Money owed to you never blocks deletion; you are free to write it off by deleting, and the App shows you the amount first so the choice is an informed one.

8. Security

We protect your data with:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between your device and our servers
  • Passwords hashed using industry-standard algorithms (never stored in plain text)
  • Row-Level Security in our database, so each user can only access lists and data they have permission to see
  • Limited access to production data, only for diagnosing critical issues

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.
